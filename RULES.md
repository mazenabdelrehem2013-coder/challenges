# Game rules

## Who plays
- **Employees** challenge colleagues, teams, departments, branches or regions, accept or decline challenges, log progress, and earn points and badges.
- **Managers** see their own department in the dashboard, approve or reject challenges created by their people (up to department level), check proof, and pick winners for manager-judged challenges.
- **HR** sees the whole company, approves branch and region challenges, runs official challenges with rewards, manages people and the org chart, and exports reports.
- **Admins** can do everything HR can, and can create other HR and admin accounts.

## Challenge levels
A challenge is between two or more sides of the same level:

| Level | A side is | Who it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Person | one employee | just them |
| Team | a team | everyone in the team |
| Department | a department | everyone in the department, in all branches |
| Branch | a branch | everyone in the branch, in all departments |
| Region | a region | everyone in the branches of that region |

## How a challenge runs
1. Someone creates it: a title, a type (fitness, learning, work goal, custom), the opponents, an end date, points at stake and how the winner is decided.
2. **Approval.** By default, department, branch and region challenges created by employees need approval (HR can change this to "all" or "none"). Managers approve up to department level; branch and region go to HR.
3. **Accepting.** Person and team challenges wait until every opponent accepts; anyone in an invited team can accept for it. Group challenges start as soon as they are approved. Official challenges from managers or HR start immediately.
4. **Progress.** Participants log numbers (steps, tickets, books...). With *photo proof* judging each entry needs an https link and a manager's OK before it counts.
5. **Finish.** After the end date the challenge is scored the next time a manager or HR has the dashboard open (it checks every minute). *Manager*-judged challenges are closed by a manager who picks the winner.

## Scoring
- A person's score is their total.
- A group's score is the **exact total** of what its members logged, and everyone can see each member's exact score.
- The highest score wins. A tie at the top shares the points. If nobody logged anything, it's a draw with no winner.

## Points
- Everyone who took part gets **10 points** for finishing (configurable).
- Each member of the winning side who logged progress gets the challenge's points. In a person challenge both people always count as taking part.
- Employees can stake at most **500 points** in a challenge they create (configurable). Official challenges have no cap and can add a real reward, like an extra day off.

## Levels
Every 200 points is a level: Rookie (1), Contender (3), Competitor (5), Challenger (7), Champion (10), Legend (15).

## Badges
| Badge | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| First win | Win a challenge |
| Hat trick | Win 3 in a row |
| Streak master | Win 7 in a row |
| Team player | Win a team, department, branch or region challenge |
| Giant slayer | Beat someone who had more points than you |
| Instigator | Start 10 challenges |
| Marathoner | Finish 25 challenges |

## Leaderboards
Rank people by points earned today, this week, this month or all time. Teams, departments, branches and regions rank by the total points of their members.
