Privacy policy

Challenges app and dashboard · Last updated 13 October 2026

Challenges is a workplace app where employees take part in friendly challenges and earn points. Your employer runs it for its staff. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Your profile: the name you type in, your team (and so your department, branch and region), and your role.
  • Game data: the challenges you create or join, the numbers you log, any optional note or proof link you add, your points, badges and win/loss record.
  • Sign-in: employees sign in without an email, using an anonymous ID created on the device. Managers and HR sign in with an email address and password.
  • Notifications and activity inside the app, such as "you were challenged" or "you won".

What we do not do

  • No ads, no selling or sharing of your data with advertisers.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data is collected.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Who can see it

Your name, team, points and challenge results are visible to colleagues in the app, for example on leaderboards. Managers and HR at your company can see participation and results in the dashboard and approve or reject challenges and logged numbers.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), protected by access rules so that only signed-in members of your company can read it. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

How long we keep it

Data is kept while your company uses the app. When you leave or ask for removal, HR can deactivate your account, and your data can be deleted on request.

Deleting your data

To have your account and data deleted, ask your company's HR team. They can deactivate your account in the dashboard straight away and arrange full deletion of your profile, entries and points.

Children

The app is meant for employees and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
